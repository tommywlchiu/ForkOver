# Attribution for the real receipt photos

The 12 photos below are third-party images used as eval fixtures under the license each one
carries. They are not covered by the repository's code license. Each is listed with its author,
license, source page, and the changes made to it.

Changes common to every file: rotated upright from its EXIF orientation, resized so the long edge
is at most 1568 px (images already smaller keep their size), re-encoded as JPEG at quality 0.85,
and stripped of metadata. Images under a CC BY-SA license are adaptations and are shared under the
same license as the original.

The `.expected.json` answer files are this project's own transcriptions, not part of the images.
